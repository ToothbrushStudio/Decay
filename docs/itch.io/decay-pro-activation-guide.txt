Thank you for buying Decay Pro.

Your activation key is on the itch.io download page for Decay: open the project from your itch library and look under "External keys". Copy the whole key, starting with DECAY1.

GUI: open Decay.App.exe, go to the License tab, paste the key, and click Activate.

CLI: run  decay activate YOUR-KEY

You only need to activate once. The key works offline, with no account.

Decrypting never needs a key, and Decay images made with Pro open in the free version.
