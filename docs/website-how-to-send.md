# How to send a Decay image

A Decay image is a delicate object. It only works as the **exact original
file**. If anything re-compresses it, resizes it, or re-saves it along the
way, the message inside is gone for good, and nothing can bring it back.

The good news: sending the file is easy once you know which ways are safe.

## Safe ways to send it

**Email, as an attachment.** Attach the PNG as a file. Don't paste it into the
body of the message. On an iPhone, if Mail asks what size to send, choose
**Actual Size**.

**AirDrop, Nearby Share, or a USB drive.** These send the file untouched.

**A cloud-drive link.** Upload the PNG to Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive,
and send the share link. Your friend downloads the original file.

**Chat apps, as a file.** Most chat apps compress photos but leave files
alone. Send the PNG as a **File** or **Document**, not as a picture:

- WhatsApp: choose *Document*
- Telegram: choose *Send as File*
- Signal: attach it as a *File*
- Discord and Slack: uploaded files are usually left as they are

## Ways that destroy it

- Sending it as a **photo** in a chat app. Most compress photos.
- Posting it to **Instagram, X, Facebook, Reddit**, or any social site. They
  all re-compress images.
- **Screenshots** and screen recordings.
- Editing, cropping, adding a filter, or saving it as a **JPEG**.
- Pasting it into a document or the body of an email.

**Tip for phones:** save the image to your *Files* app and share it from there,
not from your *Photos* app. Photos apps are more likely to convert or
compress what they share.

## Want to post it online?

Social sites re-compress every image, so the copy people see there can't be
decrypted. Instead, post the image as a **teaser**, and put a **link to the
original file** (a Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive share link) in your bio
or caption. People download the original from the link and open it in Decay.

Keep your own copy of the file. If the link ever disappears, the original is
the only way to read the message.

## If an image won't open

1. **Check the passphrase.** Spelling, capital letters, and spaces all matter.
2. **Make sure you have the original file**, not a preview, a thumbnail, or a
   screenshot.
3. **Ask the sender to send it again** as a file attachment or a cloud-drive
   link.

Decay can't tell a wrong passphrase from an image that has been changed, so
both look the same. That's by design: it's what keeps the message private.

## Good to know

- **Decrypting is always free.** You never need to buy anything to open an
  image someone sent you.
- **Nothing is uploaded.** Decay works entirely on your device. There are no
  accounts and no servers, so we can't recover a lost message or passphrase.
